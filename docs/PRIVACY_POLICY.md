# Privacy Policy: Namma Travel Companion

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Namma Travel Companion ("the app") is built so that your data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data.

## What the app uses, and why

| Data | When | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Precise location** | Only while you run the GPS meter or Night Guardian (a "Trip in progress" notification is shown) | Stays on the phone. It leaves the phone **only** when you tap **Send SOS**, inside the message you send to the contact or app you choose. |
| **Camera** | Only while Meter Cam or the Bus & Sign Lens is open | Frames are read on the device to recognise text and immediately discarded. No photos or video are saved or uploaded. |
| **Microphone** | Only while you hold the mic button in Kannada Sathi | Passed to your phone's speech recognition service (for most phones, Google's), which may process it online under Google's privacy policy. The app keeps no recording. |
| **Text you type to translate** | When you translate a custom phrase | Sent to the Google Translate API to get the Kannada translation. |
| **Place names you look up** | When you search in Travel Intel | Sent to Wikipedia to fetch a summary and photo. |
| **Emergency contact number** | If you save one in Night Guardian | Stored on the phone only. |
| **Saved places, free-use counters** | As you use the app | Stored on the phone only. |
| **Purchase** | If you buy Full Access | Handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you own Full Access. |

Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo for fixed regional points (for example Mysuru, Udupi); your location is not
sent. Destination photos are loaded from public image hosts (Wikimedia, Unsplash), which see your IP address as
with any web request.

## Sharing
The app does not sell or share personal data. Data leaves the phone only in the cases listed above, each started
by you.

## Backups
Android may back up your saved places and emergency contact to your Google account if you have device backup
turned on. Purchase data is restored by Google Play, not from backup.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Namma Travel Companion → Storage.
